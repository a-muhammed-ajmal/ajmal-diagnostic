# Muhammed Ajmal Consulting - Commercial Path and Offer Names v1.0

Status: Active working specification  
Date: August 15, 2026  
Owner: Muhammed Ajmal Consulting  
Purpose: Define the customer-facing names, role, handoff, and approved copy for the five-stage commercial path.

## 1. Decision

The public offer architecture is finalised as follows:

```text
Founder Dependency Index
  The global measurement framework and formal score.

Business Health Check
  The free customer entry experience that returns the Founder Dependency Index.
      →
Business Clarity Audit
  The evidence-led assessment that verifies the pattern and identifies the binding constraint.
      →
Focused Improvement Sprint
  A contained intervention addressing the one verified priority.
      →
Business System Build
  The larger engagement that installs the operating structure.
      →
Growth Partner Retainer
  Ongoing support that holds the standard and extends the system.
```

The commercial path is one progression, not a menu. A stage is recommended because the preceding evidence supports it, not because the founder selects a disconnected service from a list.

## 4. Stage definitions and handoffs

### 01. Business Health Check

**Customer promise:** Receive your Founder Dependency Index and see where daily operations may still rely too heavily on you.

**What the founder does:** Completes the focused self-report.

**What the founder receives:** An FDI score, component scores, reported-behaviour findings, and the limitation explaining that the result does not establish root cause.

**What it does not do:** It is not a full business, finance, tax, legal, compliance, or performance assessment. It does not identify the binding constraint or prescribe the intervention.

**Move forward when:** The founder wants the reported pattern tested against real business evidence.

**CTA:** Start the Business Health Check

### 02. Business Clarity Audit

**Customer promise:** Test the reported picture against the way the business actually operates and identify what should be addressed first.

**What the work examines:** Relevant records, workflows, dashboards, decision samples, SOPs, roles, rework, and operational information required to test the reported pattern.

**What the founder receives:** An evidence-led view of the full picture, a priority order, and the identified binding constraint.

**Move forward when:** One material priority is clear enough to improve in a focused, contained engagement.

**CTA:** Discuss a Business Clarity Audit

### 03. Focused Improvement Sprint

**Customer promise:** Improve one verified operating constraint without starting a full operating-system build.

**What the work does:** Designs and implements a practical improvement around the single binding constraint established through the Audit.

**What the founder receives:** A defined improvement delivered within the agreed scope, together with the working routines, ownership, or documentation needed to sustain it.

**Move forward when:** The business needs a connected operating structure rather than an isolated improvement.

**CTA:** Explore a Focused Improvement Sprint

### 04. Business System Build

**Customer promise:** Install the operating structure that reduces reliance on founder memory, constant decisions, and status chasing.

**What the work can include:** Strategy, systems, people, applied AI, and accountability. The exact build is determined by the Audit and agreed scope; it is not a pre-set package of tools.

**What the founder receives:** Working systems such as clear roles, decision rights, SOPs, management rhythm, dashboards, and useful automation where the process is understood.

**Move forward when:** The business needs ongoing support to hold the standard, improve the system, or extend into new capacity, offers, or markets.

**CTA:** Discuss a Business System Build

### 05. Growth Partner Retainer

**Customer promise:** Maintain the operating standard while extending the system as the business grows.

**What the work does:** Reviews the operating rhythm, resolves emerging friction, reinforces ownership and visibility, and supports agreed extensions of the system.

**What the founder receives:** Ongoing operating discipline and practical support, scoped through the agreed monthly engagement.

**CTA:** Discuss Growth Partner Support

## 6. Naming controls

### Business Health Check

Always pair the name with at least one of the following nearby:

- "Free founder-dependency self-report"
- "Receive your Founder Dependency Index"
- "Focused on decision speed, execution consistency, and operational visibility"

Never pair it with wording that implies a whole-business, financial, tax, legal, regulatory, or compliance review.

### Founder Dependency Index

Use the full name on first reference. State that it is an index out of 100, not a percentage of dependence. Do not call it a benchmark, validated diagnosis, or comprehensive health score.

### Audit

Use the full public name **Business Clarity Audit** on first reference. Explain that the Audit tests operating evidence. This prevents confusion with a statutory, financial, or tax audit.

### Sprint and Build

Describe scope only after evidence and agreement. Do not promise a list of tools, automation, templates, or commercial outcomes before the constraint and required structure are understood.

## 7. Naming-research finding

Live web research on August 15, 2026 found that "Business Health Check" is already familiar language for both free questionnaires and paid UAE advisory reviews. The same phrase is also used for broad financial, tax, compliance, and operational assessments. The name is therefore useful for instant comprehension but must carry the founder-dependency scope qualifier in this practice.

"Founder Dependency Index" is also used on other public websites. The practice can use it as its documented framework name, but this research is not a trademark or legal clearance and does not establish exclusive rights to either phrase.

Before registering a trade mark, purchasing a name-dependent domain, or claiming exclusive ownership, obtain a proper UAE and relevant international trade-mark clearance.

Research sources:

- [MyCo Business Health Check](https://app.mycentreoffice.com/business-health-check) - example of a free online business diagnostic.
- [Business Health Check UAE](https://jashvant.com/services/business-health-check) - example of a broad UAE financial and operational assessment.
- [CSP Group Business Health Check Services](https://cspgroupme.com/service/business-health-check-services/) - example of a UAE service including financial, operational, compliance, and risk work.
- [Wes Struebing frameworks](https://wesstruebing.com/frameworks) - example of another public use of the phrase Founder Dependency Index.

## 9. Quality gate

Before publishing the new names:

- Confirm every surface uses the same final stage name.
- Confirm Business Health Check appears with its FDI or focused-scope explanation.
- Confirm FDI results retain the exact limitation and the score remains `/ 100`, never `%`.
- Confirm the Audit is the only step that claims to identify the binding constraint.
- Confirm no offer is presented as a new public framework.
- Confirm the production FDI feature flag and legacy fallback before changing public CTAs.
- Complete legal clearance separately if exclusivity or registration is required.
